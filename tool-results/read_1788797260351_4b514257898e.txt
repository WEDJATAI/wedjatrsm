    24→## Order money model
    25→
    26→- subtotal = Σ(item.quantity × unitPrice)
    27→- discount (absolute EGP, 0 ≤ discount ≤ subtotal)
    28→- tax = (subtotal − discount) × TAX_RATE
    29→- total = subtotal − discount + tax
    30→- paidAmount = Σ payments; remaining = total − paidAmount
    31→- Order auto-closes (status='paid', closedAt, inventory deducted, table freed) when paidAmount ≥ total − 0.02.
